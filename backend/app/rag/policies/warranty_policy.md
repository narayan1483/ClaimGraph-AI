# ClaimGraph AI — Comprehensive Warranty Policy (WP-2026-v1)

## 1. Scope & Coverage
This Warranty Policy governs hardware protection and repair authorization for all registered consumer electronic devices, communication terminals, and portable compute equipment insured under standard or premium warranty tiers.

### 1.1 Covered Perils & Manufacturer Defects
- Internal hardware failures, mechanical switch breakdowns, and component defects arising from standard operating conditions within 12 months of certified purchase date.
- Battery degradation exceeding 20% capacity drop within the first 180 days under normal usage cycles.
- Screen digitizer and display panel failure without evidence of external point-of-impact, puncture, or liquid breach.

### 1.2 Exclusions from Standard Warranty
- Intentional modifications, unapproved third-party firmware flashing, or root-level tampering.
- Cosmetic abrasions, minor casing scratches, and normal wear and tear that do not impede device functionality.
- Liquid submersion, chemical corrosion, or water damage unless explicitly covered under the "Accidental Damage Protection (ADP)" endorsement rider.
- Devices whose factory serial numbers or IMEI tags have been defaced, altered, or duplicated.

## 2. Accidental Damage Protection (ADP) Rider
- For accounts with active ADP riders, drop impact, cracked glass, and accidental liquid spills are eligible for up to two (2) repair or replacement events per 12-month policy period.
- A mandatory standard deductible applies: $49.00 for smartphone tiers, $99.00 for laptop/tablet tiers.
- ADP claims filed within seven (7) business days of policy purchase or endorsement activation are subject to mandatory forensic receipt verification.

## 3. Repair Authorization & Authorized Facilities
- All repairs must be completed through certified OEM centers or authorized repair partner network shops.
- Unauthorized third-party repairs void the manufacturer warranty and render subsequent claims ineligible for reimbursement.
- Replacement parts must carry OEM certification tags; non-genuine component retrofitting is strictly prohibited.
