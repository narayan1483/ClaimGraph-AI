# ClaimGraph AI — Claim Escalation & Decision Authority Policy (EP-2026-v2)

## 1. Governance & Threshold Matrix
This policy dictates escalation pathways and monetary approval authorities for claim settlements, denials, and legal referrals.

| Tier | Monetary Value | Risk Level | Authorized Decision Maker |
|---|---|---|---|
| Level 1 | Up to $750.00 | LOW | Claims Specialist / Fast-track Automation |
| Level 2 | $750.01 – $2,500.00 | LOW to MEDIUM | Senior Claims Adjuster |
| Level 3 | $2,500.01 – $7,500.00 | MEDIUM to HIGH | Claims Manager / SIU Lead Investigator |
| Level 4 | Exceeding $7,500.00 | ANY, or CRITICAL Risk | Claims Vice President & Legal Counsel |

## 2. Mandatory Escalation Triggers
Regardless of claimed dollar amount, a claim must be escalated to Level 3 or higher if any of the following triggers are met:
- **ML Fraud Probability >= 0.70** or **Graph Risk Score >= 0.65**.
- **Combined Hybrid Risk Level**: Assessed as `HIGH` or `CRITICAL`.
- **Repeat Claimant History**: More than two claims within twelve (12) months.
- **Total Loss Claim on New Device**: Device registered less than fourteen (14) days prior to reported loss.
- **Repair Facility Dispute**: Allegations of fraudulent pricing or unauthorized parts installation against a network repair shop.

## 3. Human-in-the-Loop Governance Standard
- **No Autonomous Final Denial**: No insurance claim may be denied or rejected solely through automated AI or algorithmic decision.
- Every formal rejection, denial of coverage, or fraud referral requires the review, documented signature, and badge attribution of a certified human investigator or authorized claims manager.
- Claimants maintain statutory rights to appeal adverse decisions within forty-five (45) days of official notification.
