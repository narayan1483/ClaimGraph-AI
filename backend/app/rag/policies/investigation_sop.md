# ClaimGraph AI — Special Investigation Unit (SIU) Standard Operating Procedure (SOP-2026-v3)

## 1. Investigative Intake & Initial Triaging
- Every claim flagged by the hybrid intelligence engine with risk level HIGH or CRITICAL must be assigned a lead certified fraud investigator within four (4) business hours.
- The investigator must review all automated evidence, including Machine Learning anomaly attributions, Knowledge Graph relationship clusters, document forensic audits, and computer vision damage scores.

## 2. Five-Phase Investigative Protocol
### Phase 1: Identity & Policyholder Validation
- Cross-reference claimant legal name, government ID, billing address, and contact numbers against sanction screening databases and cross-carrier history.
- Verify active policy status on incident date and check for premium payment delinquencies.

### Phase 2: Device Hardware & Chain of Custody Audit
- Verify device serial number against manufacturer registration repositories, stolen property indices (e.g., GSMA blacklist), and previous carrier activations.
- If physical inspection is ordered, request device shipment to a certified forensic depot.

### Phase 3: Forensic Document & Visual Analysis
- Run visual hash matching against the historical claim damage image archive to detect recycled online photos.
- Inspect PDF invoice structures, embedded fonts, and vendor sales tax registration numbers for forgery signatures.

### Phase 4: Network Relationship & Collusion Analysis
- Inspect the Knowledge Graph for shared devices, shared payment cards, co-located policyholders, or suspicious repair vendor affiliations.
- Identify connected components exhibiting syndication characteristics.

### Phase 5: Claimant Interview & Statement Protocol
- Conduct structured telephonic or recorded statements focusing on discrepancies identified in automated analysis.
- Record claimant explanations for timeline anomalies, device storage locations, and witness corroboration.

## 3. Investigation Conclusion & Adjudication Rules
- The investigator must document findings in a signed, timestamped final investigation report.
- Supported conclusions:
  - **FOUNDED FRAUD**: Irrefutable evidence of material misrepresentation or syndicated fraud. Claim denied, referral to NICB / law enforcement.
  - **UNFOUNDED / CLEARED**: Satisfactory explanation provided for anomalies. Claim cleared for settlement.
  - **INCONCLUSIVE**: Insufficient documentation; claim held pending supplementary claimant disclosures.
