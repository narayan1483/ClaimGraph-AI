# ClaimGraph AI — Standard Claim Submission & Adjudication Policy (CP-2026-v2)

## 1. Claim Submission Window & Timelines
- All claims must be reported within thirty (30) calendar days of the alleged incident date. Claims submitted after 30 days are subject to automatic administrative rejection unless proof of medical incapacitation or natural disaster is documented.
- Claimants must provide complete incident details including date, time, physical location coordinates or municipal address, and description of the event.

## 2. Mandatory Supporting Documentation
- **Proof of Purchase**: Original retailer tax invoice or authorized order confirmation receipt showing purchaser identity, device serial number/IMEI, and purchase date.
- **Diagnostic Repair Estimate**: Itemized diagnostic assessment from an authorized service provider detailing labor rate, broken component part numbers, and estimated recovery cost.
- **Incident Photographs**: Minimum of two (2) high-resolution, unedited photographs displaying:
  1. The overall device with legible serial number or model badge.
  2. Clear close-up view of the specified physical damage or impact location.
- **Police Report**: Mandatory for all claims involving theft, burglary, robbery, or unexplained complete disappearance. Must be filed within forty-eight (48) hours of occurrence.

## 3. Claim Limits & Maximum Reimbursement
- Maximum claim reimbursement cannot exceed the original manufacturer suggested retail price (MSRP) minus statutory depreciation.
- Aggregated claims per insured device cannot exceed $3,500 within any consecutive 12-month policy cycle.
- Total replacement value for theft or complete loss is calculated based on current market equivalent value for refurbished units of identical grade.

## 4. Payment & Settlement Protocols
- Approved claims will be remitted within three (3) to five (5) business days via direct bank transfer or authorized OEM replacement voucher.
- In-kind repair settlements will be disbursed directly to the registered repair partner upon satisfactory completion of service validation.
